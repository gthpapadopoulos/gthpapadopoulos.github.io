KLEPTOTRACE, “Strengthening EU asset recovery and sanction tracing against transnational high-level corruption”, is a new security project funded by the EC Internal Security Fund. Its goal is to develop advanced artificial intelligence technologies, in order to detect high-level corruption patterns in massive amounts of data. The HUA computer vision group contributes AI and big data analytics technologies to the project.
