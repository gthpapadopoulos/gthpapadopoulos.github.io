The systematic review “Disaster Robotics in the Deep Learning Era: A Systematic Review of Platforms, Autonomy, Methods, Datasets, Challenges, and Future Research Directions” is now available. It traces the evolution of the field from teleoperated platforms to foundation-model interfaces, while proposing a multi-criteria taxonomy of response tasks, robotic platforms, perception, autonomy, as well as human-robot teaming. Additionally, it introduces a validation maturity axis and reviews the public datasets and benchmarks. The work was carried out within the TRIFFID project by G. Angelidis, A. Psiris, T. Lagkas, V. Argyriou, P. Sarigiannidis, I. Varlamis and G. Th. Papadopoulos.
