The Ceasefire project underwent its formal review for the first reporting period. The consortium demonstrated the research results for all supported use cases, including live demos of the developed modules, as well as of the integrated platform. The project received excellent comments from the external reviewers and the project officer. The next steps concern the final technological developments and their deployment in the pilot studies of the second period.
