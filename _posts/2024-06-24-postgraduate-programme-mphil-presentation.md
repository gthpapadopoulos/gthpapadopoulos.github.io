The research-oriented MPhil programme “Computer Science and Informatics” of the Department of Informatics and Telematics was presented on June 4, 2024, by its director, Prof. Iraklis Varlamis. The presentation covered the structure of the programme, its research orientation, as well as the application process. The programme mainly prepares its graduates for doctoral studies and research careers.
