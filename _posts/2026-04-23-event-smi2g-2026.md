G. Th. Papadopoulos participated in the SMI2G (Security Mission Information & Innovation Group) Event 2026 in Paris. The event aims at discussing innovative ideas and creating connections for the forthcoming calls of Horizon Europe Cluster 3 on “Civil Security for Society”. It also offered a valuable opportunity for building new partnerships, as well as for strengthening existing collaborations with European security research stakeholders.
