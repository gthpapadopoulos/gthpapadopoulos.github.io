G. Th. Papadopoulos visited the Robotics lab of the Department of Computer Science at Rutgers University in New Brunswick, NJ. During the visit, he gave an invited talk on “General Purpose Robotics: Leveraging the Power of Foundation Models”. The visit also included impressive facilities and cool demos, as well as many interesting discussions. Special thanks go to Prof. Kostas Bekris for hosting the visit, while future synergies are expected.
