The Department of Informatics and Telematics of Harokopio University accepts applications to the “MPhil in Computer Science and Informatics” for the academic year 2026-2027. The research-oriented programme targets graduates of science and technology departments, who wish to specialize in research on cutting-edge technologies. It mainly prepares its graduates for doctoral studies (PhD), as well as for careers in research and development.
