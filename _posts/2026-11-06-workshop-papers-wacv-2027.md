Papers on remote sensing and aerial video understanding were accepted at WACV 2027 workshops. “SALT: State-Aligned Low-Rank Transfer for Single-Temporal Cross-Dataset Structural Localization in Remote Sensing” bridges cross-dataset domain gaps through state-aligned low-rank adaptation, without relying on multi-temporal paired data. In parallel, “The Gap Is in the Geometry: A Convergent Meta-Analysis of Multi-Facet Aerial and Remote Sensing Video Understanding” shows that geometric variations are the main performance bottleneck of current methods. Both works were carried out within the TRIFFID project.
