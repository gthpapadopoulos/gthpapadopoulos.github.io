The article “The invisible arms race: digital trends in illicit goods trafficking and AI-enabled responses” was published in the IEEE Transactions on Technology and Society. The work provides a nice example of inter-disciplinary research, while being a key scientific outcome of the Ceasefire project. It analyzes the digital trends in illicit trafficking, as well as the AI-enabled responses that can support law enforcement.
