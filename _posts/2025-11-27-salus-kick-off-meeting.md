The kick-off meeting of the new security research project SALUS took place in Heraklion, Crete, hosted by the project coordinator Hellenic Mediterranean University. SALUS concerns “Strengthening law enforcement with advanced IoT forensic tools and enriched investigation schemes, realised by a Software Defined Network Security-as-a-Service architecture”. The HUA computer vision group mainly develops robust and explainable AI-empowered information processing pipelines, as well as Digital Twin entity and process modelling.
