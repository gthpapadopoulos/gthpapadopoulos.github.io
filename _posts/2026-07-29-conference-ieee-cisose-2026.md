G. Th. Papadopoulos and I. Varlamis participated in the IEEE CISOSE 2026 conference, hosted by the Fukuoka Institute of Technology in Fukuoka, Japan. Joint works were presented on facial affect analysis for service-oriented systems, as well as on sustainable face recognition on low-power devices with VQ-VAE embeddings. The first one reviews the advances, challenges and future visions of facial affect analysis, while the second one targets energy-efficient face recognition. Both works were supported by the TORNADO project.
