G. Th. Papadopoulos will deliver a plenary speech at the EEITE 2024 conference, entitled “Deployment of computer vision technologies in the public security domain”. The talk mainly focuses on the computer vision research outcomes of the Department of Informatics and Telematics. These concern large-scale (Big Data) visual analysis, as well as X-ray image analysis, achieved within security research projects.
