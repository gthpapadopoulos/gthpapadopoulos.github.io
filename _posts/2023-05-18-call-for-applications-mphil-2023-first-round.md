The Department of Informatics and Telematics of Harokopio University offers the research-oriented “MPhil in Computer Science and Informatics” postgraduate programme. Its main objectives are the promotion of specialized knowledge, the development of research skills, as well as the preparation for doctoral studies. The application deadline is June 23, 2023. The programme mainly addresses graduates who wish to pursue a research career.
