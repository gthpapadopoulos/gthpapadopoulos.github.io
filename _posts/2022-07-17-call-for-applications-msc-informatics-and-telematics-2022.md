The MSc programme “Informatics and Telematics” of the Department of Informatics and Telematics of Harokopio University accepts applications. The programme addresses graduates who wish to extend their studies in the area of informatics and telematics, offering specialization in modern computing technologies. Moreover, it prepares its graduates for careers in industry and research, in areas such as networks, software systems and data analytics.
