Dr. Maria Vakalopoulou, Assistant Professor at Paris-Saclay University, France, will visit the Department of Informatics and Telematics for an invited talk on “Computational methods for more accurate and precise digital pathology processing”. The talk takes place on Tuesday, December 2, at 18:00 (Greece time), in Room 3.7 on the 3rd floor of the department, at 9 Omirou Str., Tavros. Remote connection is also possible.
