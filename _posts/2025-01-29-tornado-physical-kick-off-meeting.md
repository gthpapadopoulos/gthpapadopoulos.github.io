The kick-off meeting of the TORNADO project (foundaTion mOdels for Robots that haNdle smAll, soft and Deformable Objects) took place in Athens, Greece, hosted by ITML. G. Th. Papadopoulos serves as the deputy project coordinator. TORNADO develops an adaptive cloud robotics platform for autonomous mobile robots in complex, human-populated indoor environments. Particular attention is given to manipulating small, soft or deformable objects, as well as to natural interaction with humans, by exploiting robotic foundation models.
