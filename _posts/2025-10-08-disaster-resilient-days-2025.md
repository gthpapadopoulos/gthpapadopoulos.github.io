G. Th. Papadopoulos participated in the “Disaster Resilient Days” event in Athens, Greece, organized under the framework of CERIS and the European Commission. Policymakers, experts, researchers, citizens and practitioners discussed the response to critical disaster incidents. Moreover, he spoke at the panel “Robotics in Action: Building Resilience for the Next Disaster”, representing the TRIFFID project as its coordinator. The panel focused on how current robotic technologies can facilitate first responders on the field.
