The 11th IEEE International Conference on Big Data Computing Service & Machine Learning Applications (IEEE BigDataService 2025) will take place on July 21-24, 2025, in Tucson, Arizona. G. Th. Papadopoulos serves as a program committee member, while I. Varlamis serves as general chair. The conference calls for papers and workshop proposals on big data, machine learning and AI, as well as large language models.
