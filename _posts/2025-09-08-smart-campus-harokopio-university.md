Harokopio University became the first Smart Campus in Greece, through an initiative of the Department of Informatics and Telematics. The Smart Campus @ DIT initiative introduces innovative digital services and infrastructures within the university premises. The initiative was also presented by the national press, highlighting the innovative activities of the university in the area of digital transformation.
