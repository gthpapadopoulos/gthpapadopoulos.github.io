The Istituto Italiano di Tecnologia (IIT) and Harokopio University of Athens offer fully funded PhD candidate positions in robotics, within an international joint PhD program. The positions are offered by Dr. Arash Ajoudani, Director of the HRI² laboratory, and G. Th. Papadopoulos, Head of the HUA computer vision group. The topics include multimodal learning for policy generalization and intention prediction in human-robot collaboration. Applications are sent by e-mail to the two supervisors.
