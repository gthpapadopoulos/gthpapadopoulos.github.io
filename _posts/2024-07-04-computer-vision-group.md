A student computer vision team was created at the Department of Informatics and Telematics, consisting of undergraduate students from all years, as well as postgraduate students. The team works on practical computer vision and artificial intelligence projects, in close collaboration with the HUA computer vision group. The initiative supports early-stage researchers, offering hands-on experience with modern AI technologies.
