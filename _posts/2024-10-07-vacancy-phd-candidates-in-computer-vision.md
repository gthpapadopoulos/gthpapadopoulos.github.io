The Department of Informatics and Telematics of Harokopio University of Athens offers multiple positions for PhD candidates in computer vision with artificial intelligence and deep learning methods. The selected candidates will receive funding from relevant European research projects. The theses concern cutting-edge topics of computer vision, while the candidates will work within the HUA computer vision group. Applications are sent by e-mail to G. Th. Papadopoulos.
