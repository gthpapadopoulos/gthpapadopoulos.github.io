The research-oriented MPhil programme “Computer Science and Information Technology” of the Department of Informatics and Telematics accepts applications. The programme addresses candidates who are interested in delving into specific cutting-edge research topics, or in preparing for subsequent PhD studies. More details are available on the programme website. Its main objectives include the development of research skills, as well as the preparation for doctoral studies.
