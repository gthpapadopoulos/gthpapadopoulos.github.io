The physical kick-off meeting of the EC-funded FCT project Ceasefire took place at the premises of the Centre for Research & Technology Hellas (CERTH) in Thessaloniki, Greece. G. Th. Papadopoulos serves as the technical manager of the project. Ceasefire develops efficient and reliable AI-enabled technologies to combat firearms trafficking, supporting both its offline and online aspects.
