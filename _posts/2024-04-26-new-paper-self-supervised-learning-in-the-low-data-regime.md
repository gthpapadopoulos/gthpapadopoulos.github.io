The work “Self-supervised visual learning in the low-data regime: a comparative evaluation” is now available as a preprint. Despite the straightforward conceptualization of self-supervised learning, it is not always feasible to collect or use very large pre-training datasets. The study therefore evaluates self-supervised methods in the low-data regime. It is authored by S. Konstantakos, D. I. Chalkiadaki, I. Mademlis, Y. M. Asano, E. Gavves and G. Th. Papadopoulos.
