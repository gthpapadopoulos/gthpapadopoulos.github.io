Harokopio University of Athens offers a new vacancy for a PhD candidate, for candidates interested in artificial intelligence and machine/deep learning research. The selected candidate will work on neural network methods within the HUA computer vision group, while being funded by EU research projects. The position offers the opportunity to conduct doctoral research on cutting-edge topics, in close collaboration with European research partners. Applications are sent by e-mail to G. Th. Papadopoulos.
