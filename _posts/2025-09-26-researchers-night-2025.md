The HUA computer vision group participated in the “Researcher's Night 2025” event at the National Technical University of Athens on Friday, September 26. The group members showcased live demos of the research activities within the TRIFFID and TORNADO projects. These concerned Earth observation data analysis for disaster response, human gesture and emotion recognition for human-robot interaction, as well as visual foundation models for environment perception.
