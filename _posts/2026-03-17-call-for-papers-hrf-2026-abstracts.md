The 1st Hellenic Robotics Forum (HRF 2026), organized by the HERON Hellenic Robotics Center of Excellence, accepts abstract submissions. The forum will take place on July 1-3, 2026, at the Eugenides Foundation in Palaio Faliro, Athens, Greece. The abstract submission deadline is April 15, 2026, while the author notification is expected on April 30, 2026. The forum is supported by the HUA computer vision group.
