The Department of Informatics and Telematics of Harokopio University of Athens offers multiple positions for PhD candidates and post-doctoral researchers in computer vision, using artificial intelligence and deep learning methods. The selected candidates will receive funding from relevant European research projects, while working within the HUA computer vision group. Applications are sent by e-mail to G. Th. Papadopoulos.
