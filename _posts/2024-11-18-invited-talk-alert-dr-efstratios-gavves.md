Dr. Efstratios Gavves, Associate Professor at the University of Amsterdam, will visit the Department of Informatics and Telematics for an invited talk on “Grounding Foundation Models in Reality with Physics- and Causality-informed World Models”. The talk takes place on Thursday, November 21, at 19:00 (Greece time), in the auditorium on the 1st floor of the department, at 9 Omirou Str., Tavros. Remote connection is also possible.
