G. Th. Papadopoulos co-chaired, together with P. Sarigiannidis and C. Symvoulidis, the “2nd workshop on Big Data Applications for Fight against Crime and Terrorism” at IEEE Big Data 2024 in Washington, D.C. The workshop was supported by the Ceasefire project. Moreover, multiple co-authored papers were presented, investigating data collection, processing and visualization in security applications, from entity extraction to vulnerability assessment.
