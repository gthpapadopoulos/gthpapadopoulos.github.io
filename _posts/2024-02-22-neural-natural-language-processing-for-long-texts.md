The survey “Neural natural language processing for long texts: A survey on classification and summarization” was published in Elsevier Engineering Applications of Artificial Intelligence. The work provides an introductory exploration of document analysis using modern deep neural networks. Moreover, it addresses the primary challenges of long-text classification and summarization, together with the methods that tackle them.
