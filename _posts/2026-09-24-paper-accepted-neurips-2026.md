The paper “GATE-AD: Graph Attention Network Encoding For Few-Shot Industrial Visual Anomaly Detection” was accepted at the NeurIPS 2026 Main Track as a poster presentation. The work introduces a reconstruction-based approach to few-shot industrial visual anomaly detection, which relies on a graph attention network encoder. It was carried out by the PhD student A. Psiris, together with Y. Panagakis and M. Vakalopoulou from the Archimedes Research Unit, as well as G. Th. Papadopoulos, within the TORNADO project.
