G. Th. Papadopoulos participated in the Security Research Event SRE 2025, organized by DG HOME in Warsaw. The Ceasefire project presented its technologies and live demos for tackling firearms trafficking at a dedicated booth. In particular, the HUA computer vision group demonstrated its AI tools for X-ray imaging and dark web monitoring. The event also provided a nice occasion for discussions with partners and friends.
