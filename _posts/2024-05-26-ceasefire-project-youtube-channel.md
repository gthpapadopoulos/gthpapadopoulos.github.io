The Ceasefire project launched its own YouTube channel. The channel presents videos of the project technologies, use-case demonstrations and dissemination activities, regarding the fight against illicit firearms trafficking with artificial intelligence. It mainly addresses law enforcement practitioners, researchers, as well as the broader public interested in security technologies. Moreover, the channel complements the project website and social media accounts, in order to increase the visibility of the project results.
