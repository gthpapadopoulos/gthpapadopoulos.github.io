The IEEE International Conference on AI Standardization and Quality Assurance (IEEE AI STANDARD 2025) will take place on May 5-7, 2025, in Santa Clara, California, with physical and remote participation. The conference is co-located with the IEEE Conference on Artificial Intelligence (IEEE CAI 2025). G. Th. Papadopoulos serves as co-chair of the “Computer vision” vertical, while I. Varlamis serves as program committee co-chair.
