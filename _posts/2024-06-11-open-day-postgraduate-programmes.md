The School of Digital Technology of Harokopio University, in collaboration with the Career Office, organized a hybrid open day on its postgraduate programmes on June 4, 2024. The online event presented the postgraduate programmes of the Department of Informatics and Telematics, as well as their application processes. Moreover, it offered prospective students the chance to ask questions to the programme directors.
