Within the Anti-FinTer project, Asterios Leonidis from the Foundation for Research and Technology - Hellas (FORTH) gave a presentation on “Follow-the-actor - financial investigation strategy and use of visual analytics”. The presentation discussed how visual analytics can support financial investigations of online terrorism financing. Anti-FinTer develops artificial intelligence investigative technologies for revealing online cross-border financing activities of terrorism, with G. Th. Papadopoulos as its technical manager.
