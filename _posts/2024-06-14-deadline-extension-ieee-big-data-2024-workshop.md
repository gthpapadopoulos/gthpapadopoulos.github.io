The submission deadline of the “2nd workshop on Big Data applications for fight against crime and terrorism” at IEEE Big Data 2024 was extended to October 1, 2024. The conference will take place on December 15-18, 2024, in Washington DC, USA, while remote participation is possible. The workshop is supported by the Ceasefire project and the HUA computer vision group.
