The review “Foundation Models in Robotics: A Comprehensive Review of Methods, Models, Datasets, Challenges and Future Research Directions” was accepted at Transactions on Machine Learning Research (TMLR). It analyzes the shift of robotics from fixed, single-task, domain-specific solutions towards adaptive, general-purpose agents, which are powered by foundation models. The article is authored by A. Psiris, V. Argyriou, E. Markakis, P. Sarigiannidis, E. Gavves, K. Bekris, A. Ajoudani and G. Th. Papadopoulos, within the TORNADO project.
