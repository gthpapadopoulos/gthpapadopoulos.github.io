On April 8 and 10, members of the HUA computer vision group participated in the “EpilegoCharokopio2025” event, organized by the Career Office of the university for secondary school students. The members presented scientific outcomes of the Ceasefire and TORNADO projects. The booth of the group included demos of computer vision applications, such as object detection, image classification and foundation model inference, which attracted the interest of many students.
