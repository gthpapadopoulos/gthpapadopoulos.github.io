Harokopio University received a Dell PowerEdge XE9680 server with NVIDIA H200 GPUs, as well as a Dell PowerEdge XE8640 server with NVIDIA H100 GPUs. These state-of-the-art systems will support the research of the university in artificial intelligence, driving innovation and the creation of new knowledge. The acquisition is part of the “Universities of Excellence” project, prepared by a dedicated team of the department.
