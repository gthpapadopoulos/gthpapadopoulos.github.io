The 2-day seminar “Modern technologies for combating terrorism financing” will take place on February 7-8, 2023, in Athens, Greece. The event is organized within the ISFP project Anti-FinTer, together with the sister project CTC. Multiple relevant European projects and initiatives have already confirmed their participation, while the seminar is open to researchers, practitioners and industry.
