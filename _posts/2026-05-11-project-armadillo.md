The HUA computer vision group, together with D. Michail, participates in the ARMADILLO EU project. The group contributes computer vision technologies for Raman spectra analysis, which target the detection of Gamma-Hydroxybutyrate (GHB) substances in urine, saliva and beverages. The project was also discussed at a working meeting of the Deputy Minister of Social Cohesion and Family, E. Rapti, with P. Petrou from NCSR “Demokritos” and the project management team.
