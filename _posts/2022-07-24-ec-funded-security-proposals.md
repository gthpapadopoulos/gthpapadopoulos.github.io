In the recent call for EC-funded security proposals, G. Th. Papadopoulos was involved in successful proposals, namely Ceasefire (FCT-01-10) on combating illicit firearms trafficking, TENSOR (FCT-01-05) on reliable biometric technologies for police authorities, as well as ONELAB (DRS-01-05) on mobile modular laboratories for pandemic preparedness. Moreover, he coordinated the preparation activities of the Ceasefire proposal.
