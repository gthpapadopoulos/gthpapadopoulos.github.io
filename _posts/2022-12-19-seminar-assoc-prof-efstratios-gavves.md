Within the weekly research seminars of the postgraduate programme of the Department of Informatics and Telematics, Assoc. Prof. Efstratios Gavves gave an online lecture in Greek on “The Machine Learning of Time and Dynamics for Vision, Sciences, Engineering”. The lecture took place on Thursday, December 22, at 17:30 (Greece time), with free online participation. The lecture presented recent machine learning methods for modelling time and dynamics in computer vision and scientific applications.
