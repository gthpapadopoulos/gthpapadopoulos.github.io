The IEEE International Workshop on Computer Aided Modeling and Design of Communication Links and Networks (CAMAD 2026) will take place on September 7-9, 2026, in Nicosia, Cyprus. The submission deadline is April 24, 2026. Indicative topics include beyond-5G and 6G networks, digital-twin-driven networks and AI-native networking, as well as federated and distributed learning for edge intelligence.
