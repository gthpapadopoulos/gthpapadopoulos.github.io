The review “Advances in Diffusion Models for Image Data Augmentation: A Review of Methods, Models, Evaluation Metrics and Future Research Directions” is now available as a preprint. Image data augmentation enhances the diversity and quality of training datasets, improving the performance and robustness of machine learning models. The study systematically reviews diffusion-model-based approaches for this purpose. It is authored by P. Alimisis, I. Mademlis, P. Radoglou-Grammatikis, P. Sarigiannidis and G. Th. Papadopoulos.
