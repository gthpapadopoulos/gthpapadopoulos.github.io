G. Th. Papadopoulos gave an invited talk at the FMR 2025 workshop on “Foundation Models in Robotics”, organized by the GRVC robotics lab at the Universidad de Sevilla. The talk concerned “Learning methodologies for robotic foundation models”. In particular, it summarized the research of the HUA computer vision group, including the work of A. Psiris, within the TRIFFID and TORNADO projects.
