The IEEE Big Data Service 2023 conference calls for papers on big data, machine learning and AI technologies. The conference is co-located with the IEEE International Congress on Intelligent and Service-Oriented Systems Engineering (CISOSE). It will take place on July 17-20, 2023, hosted at Harokopio University in Athens, Greece. The conference provides an excellent opportunity for the Greek and international big data community.
