The article “Resource Efficient Federated LoRaWAN Architecture for Far-Edge IoT Applications” was accepted at IEEE Access. The study introduces a resource-efficient architecture, which incorporates advanced technologies in order to create a lightweight, flexible and scalable solution for far-edge IoT applications. The article is authored by A. Triantafyllou, I. Siniosoglou, V. Argyriou, S. K. Goudos, G. Th. Papadopoulos, K. Panitsidis and P. Sarigiannidis.
