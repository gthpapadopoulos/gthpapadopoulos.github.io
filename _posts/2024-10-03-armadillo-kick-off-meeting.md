The kick-off meeting of the ARMADILLO project took place on October 3-4, hosted by NCSR “Demokritos” in Athens, Greece. ARMADILLO develops a holistic, easy-to-use, portable toolset that rapidly detects concentrations of GHB substances in urine, saliva and beverages, in order to prevent drug-facilitated violence and assault. The HUA computer vision group develops artificial intelligence technologies for Raman spectra analysis, as well as data fusion.
