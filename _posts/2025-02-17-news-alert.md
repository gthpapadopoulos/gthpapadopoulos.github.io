Part of the work on handling data heterogeneity in federated learning-based intrusion detection systems was adopted as a new baseline by Flower Labs. The work is the StatAvg method, authored by P. S. Bouzinis, P. Radoglou-Grammatikis, I. Makris, T. Lagkas, V. Argyriou, G. Th. Papadopoulos, P. Sarigiannidis and G. K. Karagiannidis. Particular thanks go to P. Radoglou-Grammatikis and P. Sarigiannidis for leading the initiative.
