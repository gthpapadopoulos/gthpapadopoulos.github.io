Applications are open for the updated MSc programme of the Department of Informatics and Telematics on “Advances in Computer Science and Information Systems”. The programme supports specializations in data science and artificial intelligence, cloud/edge systems and applications, as well as information systems management and digital transformation. Moreover, an open day event took place on May 6 at the department.
