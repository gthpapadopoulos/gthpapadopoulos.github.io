Dr. Efstratios Gavves, Associate Professor at the University of Amsterdam, visited the Department of Informatics and Telematics for an invited talk on “Grounding Foundation Models in Reality with Physics- and Causality-informed World Models”. The lecture attracted increased participation of students and researchers. Moreover, it stimulated fruitful discussions and an interesting Q&A session, while further synergies are expected.
