The video below presents a platform for large-scale photorealistic outdoor 3D scene reconstruction, which combines Unity augmented reality with Gaussian splatting. The solution reconstructs complex large-scale scenes in near real time, processing only RGB video streams from aerial drones. Moreover, it achieves up to millimetre spatial resolution, while it integrates into Unity for AR/VR visualization on desktop, headset and mobile platforms.
