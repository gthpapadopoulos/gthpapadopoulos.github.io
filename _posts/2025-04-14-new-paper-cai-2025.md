The white paper “State of play and future directions in industrial computer vision AI standards” was accepted at the IEEE AI Standard 2025 conference, co-located with the IEEE Conference on Artificial Intelligence (CAI 2025). The recent advancements in AI and deep learning raise the need for standards that support the industrial adoption of computer vision. The work is authored by A. Stefanidou, P. Radoglou-Grammatikis, V. Argyriou, P. Sarigiannidis, I. Varlamis and G. Th. Papadopoulos.
