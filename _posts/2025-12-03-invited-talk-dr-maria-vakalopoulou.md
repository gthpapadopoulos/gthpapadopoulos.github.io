Dr. Maria Vakalopoulou, Assistant Professor at CentraleSupélec, Paris-Saclay University, and Lead Researcher at the Archimedes Research Unit, visited the Department of Informatics and Telematics for an invited talk. The talk concerned “Computational methods for more accurate and precise digital pathology processing”. An interesting Q&A session and stimulating ideas, as well as bilateral group discussions, complemented the day, while further synergies are expected.
