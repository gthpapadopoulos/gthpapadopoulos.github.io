The Department of Informatics and Telematics of Harokopio University of Athens offers a full-time vacancy for a junior researcher position in computer vision technologies. The position focuses on the application of artificial intelligence and machine/deep learning solutions to image analysis tasks. The selected candidate will receive funding from EU research projects, while working within the HUA computer vision group.
