Within the weekly research seminars of the postgraduate programme of the Department of Informatics and Telematics, G. Th. Papadopoulos gave an online lecture in Greek on “Interpretations of computer vision methods using explainable artificial intelligence techniques”. The lecture took place on Thursday, December 8, at 17:30 (Greece time). It concentrated on explainable AI techniques that interpret the decisions of computer vision models.
