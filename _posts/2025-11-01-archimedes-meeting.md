G. Th. Papadopoulos participated in the retreat meeting of the ARCHIMEDES Research Unit on AI, Data Science, and Algorithms, held in Xylokastro, Corinth, Greece. The meeting was well prepared, presenting promising and diverse future research ideas. Moreover, interesting group discussions took place among the participating researchers, in order to identify common research directions and future collaborations.
