G. Th. Papadopoulos attended the SMI2G (Security Mission Information & Innovation Group) 2023 brokerage event, which focused on EC security proposal funding opportunities. The two-day event offered the opportunity to discuss ideas for forthcoming Horizon Europe security calls. Moreover, it supported building new partnerships, as well as strengthening existing collaborations with European security research stakeholders.
