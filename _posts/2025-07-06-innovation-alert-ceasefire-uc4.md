Within the Ceasefire project, the HUA computer vision group developed an artificial intelligence solution that automatically identifies firearm security threats, such as firearms, firearm parts and ammunition, during X-ray fast-parcel inspection by customs services. The solution achieved excellent detection performance during real-world pilot studies in operational settings. Moreover, discussions with the security ecosystem, including the Universal Postal Union, Europol and CEPOL, are ongoing for its further development and exploitation.
