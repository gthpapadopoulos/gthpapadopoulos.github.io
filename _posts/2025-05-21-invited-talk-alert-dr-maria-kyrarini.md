Dr. Maria Kyrarini, Assistant Professor at Santa Clara University, will visit the Department of Informatics and Telematics for an invited talk on “Human-Centered Assistive Robots: From Understanding Commands to Detecting Cognitive Fatigue”. The talk takes place on Friday, May 30, at 17:30 (Greece time), in Room 2.3 on the 2nd floor of the department, at 9 Omirou Str., Tavros. Remote connection is also possible.
