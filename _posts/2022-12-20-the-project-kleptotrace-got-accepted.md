The proposal KLEPTOTRACE, “Strengthening EU asset recovery and sanction tracing against transnational high-level corruption”, was accepted under the Internal Security Fund (ISF) topic ISF-2022-TF1-AG-CORRUPT. KLEPTOTRACE will boost the investigation, tracing and recovery of assets related to transnational high-level corruption. The HUA computer vision group contributes artificial intelligence technologies for detecting corruption patterns in large amounts of data.
