G. Th. Papadopoulos participated in the 1st Hellenic Robotics Forum (HRF 2026), organized by the HERON Hellenic Robotics Center of Excellence, while also serving on its organizing committee. The forum gathered leading researchers from academia and industry, in order to discuss the future of robotics in the AI era. Moreover, the HUA computer vision group supported the initiative. The event offered interesting discussions and stimulating ideas, as well as many opportunities to meet colleagues.
