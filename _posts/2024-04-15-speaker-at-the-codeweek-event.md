G. Th. Papadopoulos participated as a speaker in the Codeweek event, organized by the kariera.gr Group. The presentation concerned recent technological advancements in “Computer vision technologies in the security domain”. In particular, it summarized the research achievements of the Ceasefire project, mainly regarding X-ray image analysis and visual web analytics for law enforcement. The event mainly addressed IT professionals and students interested in modern AI applications.
