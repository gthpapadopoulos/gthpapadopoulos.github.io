G. Th. Papadopoulos was part of the organizing team of the seminar “Modern technologies for combating terrorism financing”, supported by the ISFP sister projects Anti-FinTer and CTC. Additionally, he presented the closely related Ceasefire project. He serves as the project technical manager of both Anti-FinTer and Ceasefire. The seminar included many interesting views, live demos, as well as in-depth discussions.
