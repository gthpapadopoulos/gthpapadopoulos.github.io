Applications are open for the Master of Applied GeoInformatics (MAGIC) at Harokopio University. The interdisciplinary postgraduate programme combines geography with modern information technologies, in order to support spatial analysis and management. It is co-organized by the Department of Geography and the Department of Informatics and Telematics. The application deadline is September 30, 2025. It mainly prepares its graduates for careers in geospatial data analysis and management.
