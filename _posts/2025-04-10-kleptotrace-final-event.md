The final event of the KLEPTOTRACE project took place at the Europol headquarters. Over two years, the project investigated complex corruption networks, designed investigation tools and spread knowledge among practitioners. Most importantly, it built a strong cross-border team of researchers and law enforcement experts, to which the HUA computer vision group contributed AI and data analytics technologies.
