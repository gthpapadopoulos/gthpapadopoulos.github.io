Within multiple EC-funded projects, the Department of Informatics and Telematics of Harokopio University of Athens offers a full-time vacancy for a post-doctoral researcher in computer vision. The position concerns applications in vertical domains, such as robotics, remote sensing, augmented reality and security. It is planned to be funded for up to 3 years. Applications are sent by e-mail to G. Th. Papadopoulos.
