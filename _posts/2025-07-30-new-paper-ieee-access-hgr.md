The survey “Survey on Hand Gesture Recognition from Visual Input” was accepted at IEEE Access. Hand gesture recognition has become an important research area, driven by the demand for human-computer interaction in fields such as sign language recognition. The survey reviews the main methods, datasets and challenges of vision-based hand gesture recognition. The article is authored by M. Linardakis, I. Varlamis and G. Th. Papadopoulos, within the TRIFFID and TORNADO projects.
