In preparation for the interim review of the TRIFFID project, the consortium met on April 20-21 for its physical meeting and integration days, hosted by the University of West Bohemia in Pilsen. With the first half of the project completed, the sessions focused on aligning the AI, computer vision and robotics solutions. The main activities included system integration on the actual robotic platforms, as well as field testing of the UAV and UGV systems.
