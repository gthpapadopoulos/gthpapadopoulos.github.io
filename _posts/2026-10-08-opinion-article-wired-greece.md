G. Th. Papadopoulos contributed to an opinion article in WIRED Greece on the “Ghost Font”, an experimental tool that hides text within motion in order to confuse AI models. The discussion with the editor V. Tatsiopoulos explains how such visual tricks rely on decades-old psychophysics concepts, such as random-dot kinematograms. Moreover, it explains why current multimodal AI models initially struggle to read them, since they mostly sample videos as sparse static frames rather than processing motion as a continuous signal.
