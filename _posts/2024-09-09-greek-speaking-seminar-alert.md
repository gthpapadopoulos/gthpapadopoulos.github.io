The HUA computer vision group organizes a half-day Greek-speaking event, as part of the “Research outcomes” seminar series of the Department of Informatics and Telematics, with slides in English. The seminar presents the research activities of the group and its ongoing research projects. Moreover, it presents available topics for bachelor, master and doctoral theses, as well as available research job opportunities.
