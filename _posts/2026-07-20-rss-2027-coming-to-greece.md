The Robotics: Science and Systems conference (RSS 2027) will take place in Athens, Greece, while Harokopio University of Athens will co-host it. The initiative is coordinated by Prof. Kostas Daniilidis, who made the formal announcement during RSS 2026 in Sydney. The organizing committee further includes S. Nikolaidis, G. Pappas, L. E. Kavraki, A. Argyros, G. Chalvatzaki, D. Kanoulas, P. Maragos and M. Chli. The conference provides an important opportunity for the Greek robotics community.
