The proposal SALUS, “Strengthening law enforcement with advanced IoT forensic tools and enriched investigation schemes, realised by a Software Defined Network Security-as-a-Service architecture”, was selected for funding by the EC under call HORIZON-CL3-2024-FCT-01-01. SALUS addresses the security challenges of IoT systems, providing law enforcement with new forensic investigation schemes and advanced IoT forensic tools. The HUA computer vision group focuses on robust and explainable AI-empowered information processing, as well as digital twin modelling.
