The survey “Beyond vulnerabilities: A comprehensive survey of adversarial attacks across domains” was accepted at Computer Science Review. Adversarial attacks present significant risks to machine learning systems, since they exploit model vulnerabilities and threaten the integrity, security and trustworthiness of applications. The survey reviews such attacks across multiple sectors and application domains. The article is authored by D. C. Asimopoulos, P. Radoglou-Grammatikis, G. Th. Papadopoulos and P. Sarigiannidis.
