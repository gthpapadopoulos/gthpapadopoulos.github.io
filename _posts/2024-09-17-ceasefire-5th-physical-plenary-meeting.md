The 5th physical plenary meeting of the Ceasefire project took place on September 16-17 in Heraklion, Greece, at the facilities of FORTH. The partners discussed the progress of the project, shared ideas and planned its future steps. A special highlight was the 3rd training workshop of the project, which focused on the criminal event analysis suite and its use by practitioners in complex cases.
