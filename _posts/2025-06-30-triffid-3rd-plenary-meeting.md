The 3rd General Assembly of the TRIFFID project took place in Heraklion, Greece, hosted by Hellenic Mediterranean University and the Pasiphae Lab. Partners from across Europe gathered for two days of collaboration on the user requirements, pilot use cases, data strategy, as well as technical integration. The meeting aligned the consortium on key deliverables, in order to advance smart robotic reconnaissance for search and rescue operations.
