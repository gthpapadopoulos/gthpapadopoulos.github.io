The Ceasefire project organized the online training session “Ceasefire Tools & Use Case Demonstration – Part A” on April 18. The session presented in depth the functionalities and applications of the Ceasefire tools, which support law enforcement agencies in the fight against illicit firearms trafficking. It addressed practitioners from the law enforcement community, as well as other interested stakeholders.
