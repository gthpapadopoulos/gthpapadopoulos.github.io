G. Th. Papadopoulos became a member of the editorial board of the Applied System Innovation journal, in the Human-Computer Interaction section. He contributes expertise in computer vision, artificial intelligence and machine/deep learning, as well as big data analytics, human action recognition and explainable artificial intelligence. The journal publishes research on applied systems, including human-computer interaction technologies.
