The work “A Unified AI-Driven Edge Framework for Autonomous Search and Rescue Drone Mission Planning” was presented at the RSS 2026 workshop on “Planning and Control with Imperfect Sensors and Perception” in Sydney, Australia. It introduces an integrated framework for autonomous search and rescue drones, which operate in cluttered, dynamically changing and partially known environments. The work is co-authored by C. Chronis, I. Varlamis and G. Th. Papadopoulos.
