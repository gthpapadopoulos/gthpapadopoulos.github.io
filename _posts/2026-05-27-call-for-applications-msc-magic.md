Harokopio University opened the application period for the inter-departmental MSc programme “Applied Geoinformatics” (MAGIC), co-organized by the Department of Geography and the Department of Informatics and Telematics. The programme provides in-depth theoretical and practical training at the intersection of spatial analysis and modern data science. It mainly focuses on cutting-edge areas, such as geographic information systems, remote sensing, as well as big data analytics.
