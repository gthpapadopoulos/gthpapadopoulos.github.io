G. Th. Papadopoulos participated in the SMI2G 2024 brokerage event in Paris. The two-day event focused on discussing ideas and building synergies for the upcoming EC security calls for proposals. It also offered a valuable opportunity for building new partnerships, as well as for strengthening existing collaborations with European security research stakeholders. Moreover, it offered the chance to present the security research activities of the HUA computer vision group.
