The Department of Informatics and Telematics of Harokopio University offers the research-oriented “MPhil in Computer Science and Informatics” postgraduate programme. Among its main objectives are the promotion of specialized knowledge and the development of research skills, as well as the preparation for doctoral studies. Interested candidates can find the details of the programme and the application process on its website.
