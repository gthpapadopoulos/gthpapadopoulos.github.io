The kick-off meeting of the EC-funded FCT project TENSOR, “Reliable biometric technologies to assist police authorities in combating terrorism and organized crime”, took place at the premises of Netcompany-Intrasoft in Athens, Greece. TENSOR provides law enforcement agencies with a platform for biometric evidence extraction, sharing and storage in cross-border environments. The platform works in an automated, robust, secure, privacy-preserving and scalable manner.
