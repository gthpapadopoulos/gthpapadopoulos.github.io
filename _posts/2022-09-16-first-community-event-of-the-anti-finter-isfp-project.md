The first major community event of the Anti-FinTer ISFP project took place at the premises of the Instituto de Estudios Fiscales (IEF) in Madrid, Spain. The event delivered training courses on crypto-assets and dark web analytics fundamentals, through the first Knowledge Hub Meeting of the project. Moreover, it organized a hackathon, where law enforcement practitioners competed in a “Capture the Flag” challenge using the project tools.
