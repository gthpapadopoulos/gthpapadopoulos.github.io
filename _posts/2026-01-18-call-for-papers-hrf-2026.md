The 1st Hellenic Robotics Forum (HRF 2026), organized by the HERON Hellenic Robotics Center of Excellence, will take place on July 1-3, 2026, at the Eugenides Foundation in Palaio Faliro, Athens, Greece. The submission deadline is March 30, 2026. Indicative topics include field and space robotics, healthcare and social robotics, marine robotics, as well as safety and security robotics. Moreover, multimodal robot perception, human-robot interaction, and AI for robotics are covered.
