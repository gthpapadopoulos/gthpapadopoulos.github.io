The Department of Informatics and Telematics accepts applications for the new inter-institutional postgraduate programme “Digital Health and Analytics” until September 16, 2022. The programme combines digital health services with data analytics, addressing graduates of informatics, health and related disciplines. The programme is offered in collaboration with other Greek institutions, while it prepares its graduates for the digital transformation of health services.
