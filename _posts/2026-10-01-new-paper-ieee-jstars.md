The systematic review “RGB Optical Remote Sensing in the Deep Learning Era: A Systematic Review of Methods, Models, Datasets, Challenges, and Future Research Directions” was accepted at the IEEE Journal of Selected Topics in Applied Earth Observations and Remote Sensing (JSTARS). It examines how deep learning has transformed optical remote sensing analysis, from handcrafted-feature pipelines to end-to-end learned representations, while outlining the open challenges of the field. The article is authored by P. Koletsis, G. Angelidis, V. Efthymiou, D. Michail, C. Chalkias, T. Lagkas, P. Sarigiannidis and G. Th. Papadopoulos.
