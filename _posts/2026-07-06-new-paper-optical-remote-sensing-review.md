The systematic review “RGB Optical Remote Sensing in the Deep Learning Era: A Systematic Review of Methods, Models, Datasets, Challenges, and Future Research Directions” is now available as a preprint. It analyzes how deep learning has transformed optical remote sensing analysis, shifting the field from handcrafted-feature pipelines to end-to-end learned representations. Moreover, it reviews the main methods, models and datasets, as well as the open challenges. The work is authored by P. Koletsis, G. Angelidis, V. Efthymiou, D. Michail, C. Chalkias, T. Lagkas, P. Sarigiannidis and G. Th. Papadopoulos.
