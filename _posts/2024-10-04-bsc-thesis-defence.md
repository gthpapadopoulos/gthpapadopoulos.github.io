Jorgen Cani successfully defended his BSc thesis “Scan image analysis using deep learning techniques”, supervised by G. Th. Papadopoulos. The thesis explored deep neural network architectures for fine-grained security X-ray image analysis, mainly object detection, with experiments on vision transformers, CNNs and hybrid models. During the thesis, Jorgen also contributed to multiple scientific publications on X-ray object detection, self-supervised learning and the Ceasefire system.
