G. Th. Papadopoulos participated in the EMPACT Firearms strategic meeting organized by Europol, where he presented the activities of the Ceasefire project on tackling firearms trafficking. The meeting included many interesting discussions with law enforcement practitioners and policy makers. Moreover, it opened opportunities for collaborative activities with the European firearms community. In particular, the presentation focused on the AI technologies that the HUA computer vision group develops for the project.
