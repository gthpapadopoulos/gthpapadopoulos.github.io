The article “Exploring Energy Landscapes for Minimal Counterfactual Explanations: Applications in Cybersecurity and Beyond” was published in the IEEE Transactions on Artificial Intelligence. The work presents a framework that integrates perturbation theory and statistical mechanics, in order to generate minimal counterfactual explanations in explainable AI. The article is authored by S. Evangelatos, E. Veroni, V. Efthymiou, C. D. Nikolopoulos, G. Th. Papadopoulos and P. Sarigiannidis.
