G. Th. Papadopoulos and I. Varlamis participated as program committee members in the IEEE BigDataService 2025 conference, co-located with IEEE CISOSE 2025 in Tucson, Arizona. Moreover, the work “X-ray illicit object detection using hybrid CNN-transformer neural network architectures” was presented at the conference. The work is authored by J. Cani, C. Diou, S. Evangelatos, P. Radoglou-Grammatikis, V. Argyriou, P. Sarigiannidis, I. Varlamis and G. Th. Papadopoulos, within the Ceasefire project.
