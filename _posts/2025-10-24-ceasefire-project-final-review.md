The final review meeting of the Ceasefire project took place, which tackled organized crime activities related to firearms trafficking. The project officer and the reviewers provided very positive feedback on the collaborative effort of the consortium. The HUA computer vision group served as the project technical manager, while contributing various AI technologies. These include a solution for firearm detection in X-ray fast-parcel imaging, which was tested under real operational settings.
