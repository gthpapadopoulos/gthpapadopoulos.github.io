The comparative evaluation “Self-supervised visual learning in the low-data regime: A comparative evaluation” was accepted at Elsevier Neurocomputing. The paper studies self-supervised learning methods when very large pre-training datasets cannot be collected or used. It results from a collaboration between the Department of Informatics and Telematics and the University of Amsterdam. The article is authored by S. Konstantakos, J. Cani, I. Mademlis, D. I. Chalkiadaki, Y. M. Asano, E. Gavves and G. Th. Papadopoulos.
