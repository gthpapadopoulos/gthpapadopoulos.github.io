G. Th. Papadopoulos attended the CERIS (Community for European Research and Innovation for Security) FCT workshop in Brussels on “Europol's new role in Horizon Europe”. He represented the FCT projects Ceasefire and TENSOR. The workshop discussed how Europol can support security research projects, as well as the uptake of their results by law enforcement agencies.
