The application period for the “MPhil in Computer Science and Informatics” of the Department of Informatics and Telematics was extended until September 7, 2025. The research-oriented postgraduate programme focuses on research in computer science and informatics, while full-remote attendance is possible. It mainly prepares its graduates for doctoral studies, as well as for careers in research and development. Inquiries can be sent to G. Th. Papadopoulos.
