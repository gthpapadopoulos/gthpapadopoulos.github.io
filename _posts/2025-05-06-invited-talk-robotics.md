G. Th. Papadopoulos visited the Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering at Santa Clara University, in the heart of Silicon Valley. During the visit, he gave an invited talk on “Foundation models in robotics”. The visit included fruitful discussions and outstanding facilities, as well as great hospitality from Prof. Maria Kyrarini. The talk presented the recent research of the HUA computer vision group on robotic foundation models, mainly within the TORNADO project.
