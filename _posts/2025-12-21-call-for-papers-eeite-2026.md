The 7th International Conference in Electronic Engineering & Information Technology (EEITE 2026), technically co-sponsored by the IEEE Greece Section, will take place in Chania, Crete, Greece, in June 2026. It hosts the special session “Recent advancements in robotic perception, learning, interaction, communication and control technologies”. The session is supported by the EU projects TRIFFID, TORNADO, SOPRANO, NANCY, TALON and INTACT. The provisional submission deadline is January 25, 2026.
