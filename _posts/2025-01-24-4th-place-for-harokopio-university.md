Harokopio University of Athens was ranked 4th among the Greek universities in the recent ranking based on the qualitative criteria of the Hellenic Authority for Higher Education (HAHE). The ranking reflects the quality of the teaching and research activities of the university. The HUA computer vision group is proud to contribute to these activities, as part of the Department of Informatics and Telematics.
