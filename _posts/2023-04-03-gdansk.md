G. Th. Papadopoulos visited the police warehouses of the Provincial Police Headquarters in Gdańsk, within the Ceasefire project. In collaboration with the Polish customs authorities, AI tools are developed for automatically detecting firearms trafficking in fast parcel services using X-ray technologies. The first step of this work concerns the development of real-world datasets. These datasets will support the training and evaluation of the X-ray detection models of the project.
