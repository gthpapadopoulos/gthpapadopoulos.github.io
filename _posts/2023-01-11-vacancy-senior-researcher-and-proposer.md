Harokopio University of Athens offers a vacancy for a senior researcher and proposer position, for candidates interested in cutting-edge research and development in computer vision, artificial intelligence and machine/deep learning. The position supports the research activities and proposal preparation of the HUA computer vision group. Applications are received only by e-mail to G. Th. Papadopoulos.
