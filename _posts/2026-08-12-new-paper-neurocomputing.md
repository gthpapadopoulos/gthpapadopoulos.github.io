The review “Visual hand gesture recognition with deep learning: A comprehensive review of methods, datasets, challenges and future research directions” was accepted at Elsevier Neurocomputing. It provides an overview of deep-learning-based visual hand gesture recognition, covering the main methods, datasets and evaluation practices, while discussing the open challenges and future research directions of the field. The article is authored by K. Foteinos, M. Linardakis, P. Radoglou-Grammatikis, V. Argyriou, P. Sarigiannidis, I. Varlamis and G. Th. Papadopoulos, within the TORNADO project.
