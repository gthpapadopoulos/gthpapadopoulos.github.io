Dr. Vagelis Papakonstantinou, Professor at Vrije Universiteit Brussel, will visit the Department of Informatics and Telematics for an invited talk on “The Regulation of Digital Technologies in the EU”. The talk takes place on Friday, March 28, at 17:30 (Greece time), in Room 2.3 on the 2nd floor of the department, at 9 Omirou Str., Tavros. Remote connection is also possible.
