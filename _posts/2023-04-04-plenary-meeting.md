The 2nd physical plenary meeting of the Ceasefire project took place on April 4-5 in Poland, at the premises of the Provincial Police Headquarters in Gdańsk, which is a partner of the consortium. The partners exchanged ideas and shared progress on combating firearms trafficking. Moreover, they discussed the next actions of the project, while planning its future work.
