G. Th. Papadopoulos participated in the final conference of the RESPOND-A project, hosted by the European University Cyprus, as a member of the external advisory board of the project. RESPOND-A developed technologies for supporting the operational activities of first responders. The conference presented the main project outcomes, as well as the results of its field trials.
