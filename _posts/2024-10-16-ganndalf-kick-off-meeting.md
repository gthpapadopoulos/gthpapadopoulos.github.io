The kick-off meeting of the GANNDALF project took place in Warsaw, Poland, hosted by the Polish Platform for Homeland Security. GANNDALF sets new grounds to the fight against advanced cyber threats and cyber-dependent crimes. It supports data sharing among agencies, a decision-support toolbox and a collaborative crime investigation sandbox, as well as policy drafting and training. The HUA computer vision group develops AI technologies, including graph neural networks for data processing and representation.
