Multiple European projects and initiatives confirmed their participation in the seminar “Modern technologies for combating terrorism financing”. These include Anti-FinTer, CTC, Ceasefire, STARLIGHT, CRAAFT, I-LEAD, Tools4LEAs (EACTDA), CREST, as well as the ENLETS network. Fruitful discussions are expected among researchers, law enforcement practitioners and industry. The seminar will take place on February 7-8, 2023, in Athens, Greece, within the ISFP project Anti-FinTer.
