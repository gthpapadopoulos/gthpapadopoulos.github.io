G. Th. Papadopoulos served as the academic responsible, on behalf of Harokopio University, for the Operational Intelligence Analysis Training. Law enforcement professionals from multiple EU countries completed the course, which aimed to strengthen the collective capacity of the EU to fight organized crime and terrorism. The TrainTheTrainers programme was implemented by Europol, in close cooperation with CEPOL and the Centre for Continuing Education and Lifelong Learning of Harokopio University.
