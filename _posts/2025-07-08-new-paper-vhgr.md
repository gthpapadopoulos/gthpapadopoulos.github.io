The review “Visual Hand Gesture Recognition with Deep Learning: A Comprehensive Review of Methods, Datasets, Challenges and Future Research Directions” is now available as a preprint. It provides a comprehensive overview of visual hand gesture recognition with deep learning, covering the main methods, datasets and evaluation practices. Moreover, it discusses the open challenges of the field. The work is authored by K. Foteinos, J. Cani, M. Linardakis, P. Radoglou-Grammatikis, V. Argyriou, P. Sarigiannidis, I. Varlamis and G. Th. Papadopoulos.
