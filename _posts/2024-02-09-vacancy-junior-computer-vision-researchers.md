The Department of Informatics and Telematics of Harokopio University of Athens offers full-time vacancies for junior researcher positions in computer vision technologies. The positions focus on the application of artificial intelligence and machine/deep learning solutions to image analysis tasks. The selected candidates will receive funding from EU research projects, while working within the HUA computer vision group.
