Within the ISFP project Anti-FinTer and together with the sister project CTC, a 2-day seminar on “Modern technologies for combating terrorism financing” is organized on February 7-8, 2023, in Athens, Greece. The organizers invite researchers, law enforcement practitioners, developers, SMEs and industry to participate, by presenting relevant initiatives and EC-funded projects, as speakers in panel discussions, or as attendees.
