The 6th International Conference in Electronic Engineering & Information Technology (EEITE 2025), technically co-sponsored by the IEEE Greece Section, will take place on June 4-6, 2025, in Chania, Crete, Greece. It hosts the special session “Recent advancements in robotic perception, learning, interaction, communication and control technologies”, supported by the EU projects TRIFFID, TORNADO, SOPRANO, NANCY, TALON and INTACT. The provisional submission deadline is February 9, 2025.
