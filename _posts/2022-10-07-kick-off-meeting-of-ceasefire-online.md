The online kick-off meeting of the EC-funded FCT project Ceasefire, “Advanced versatile artificial intelligence technologies and interconnected cross-sectoral fully-operational national focal points for combating illicit firearms trafficking”, took place. G. Th. Papadopoulos serves as the technical manager of the project. Ceasefire develops efficient and reliable AI-enabled technologies to combat firearms trafficking, supporting both its offline and online aspects.
