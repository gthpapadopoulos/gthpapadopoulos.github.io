With the summer vacations over, the HUA computer vision group restarted its periodic bi-weekly team meetings. The meetings review the progress of the ongoing research activities and projects, while planning the next steps of each team member. Moreover, they provide a regular forum for exchanging ideas and supporting the newer members of the group. The group wishes all its members a productive new academic year.
