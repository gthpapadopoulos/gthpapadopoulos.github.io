The article “StatAvg: Mitigating Data Heterogeneity in Federated Learning for Intrusion Detection Systems” was accepted at the IEEE Transactions on Network and Service Management. Federated learning enables devices to collaboratively build a shared model, although heterogeneous local data degrade its performance. StatAvg addresses this problem for intrusion detection systems. The article is authored by P. S. Bouzinis, P. Radoglou-Grammatikis, I. Makris, T. Lagkas, V. Argyriou, G. Th. Papadopoulos, P. Sarigiannidis and G. K. Karagiannidis.
