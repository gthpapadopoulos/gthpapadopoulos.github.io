The HUA computer vision group seeks a collaborator for the financial management of its research projects. The group conducts cutting-edge research in computer vision and artificial intelligence, while participating actively in research projects funded by Horizon Europe. The role mainly involves the financial management of the projects, as well as the communication with the Special Account for Research Funds (ELKE) of the university. Applications are sent by e-mail to G. Th. Papadopoulos.
