The 2nd plenary meeting of the TRIFFID project concluded in Rotterdam. Over the two days, the partners made important progress in defining the user requirements, strengthening the collaboration among work packages, and advancing the development of the TRIFFID system. Moreover, a visit to the operational premises of a first-responder partner provided valuable real-world context to the work of the consortium.
