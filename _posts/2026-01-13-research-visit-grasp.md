G. Th. Papadopoulos visited the GRASP laboratory at the School of Engineering of the University of Pennsylvania in Philadelphia, PA. The visit included a tour of the outstanding facilities and impressive demos, as well as many interesting discussions with the lab personnel. Special thanks go to Prof. Kostas Daniilidis for hosting the visit, while future synergies between the two groups are expected.
