G. Th. Papadopoulos gave a talk on “Foundation Models in robotics” within the Archimedes Talks Series. The talk investigated holistically the usage of foundation models in robotics. It introduced their fundamental principles and the evolution of robotic foundation model research, as well as the main learning methodologies, including pre-training, fine-tuning, reinforcement learning and imitation learning. Moreover, it outlined the robotic tasks addressed by such models.
