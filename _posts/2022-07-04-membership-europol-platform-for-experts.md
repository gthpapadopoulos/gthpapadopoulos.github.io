G. Th. Papadopoulos became a member of the Europol Platform for Experts (EPE). The platform aims at sharing knowledge and best practices within the broader EU security innovation hub and its community. The membership supports the exchange of research outcomes with law enforcement agencies, as well as the uptake of AI technologies in security. In particular, it connects researchers with practitioners of the EU security community.
