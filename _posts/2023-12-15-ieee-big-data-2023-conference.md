G. Th. Papadopoulos participated in the IEEE Big Data 2023 conference, presenting multiple works of the HUA computer vision group. He also contributed to a workshop on fighting crime and terrorism, organized by the Ceasefire project, where he further presented work of the Anti-FinTer project. The presented works include visual inspection for illicit items in X-ray images, by I. Mademlis, G. Batsis, A. A. R. Chrysochoou and G. Th. Papadopoulos.
