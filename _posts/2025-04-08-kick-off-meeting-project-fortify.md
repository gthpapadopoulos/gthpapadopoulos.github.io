G. Th. Papadopoulos was invited to the kick-off meeting of the ISF project FORTIFY in Madrid, in order to represent the Ceasefire project and its outcomes. FORTIFY establishes a global community to address the threat of firearms trafficking. High-level experts from EMPACT Firearms, Europol, Interpol, CEPOL, DG HOME, as well as police and customs officers, participated in the meeting. The HUA computer vision group demonstrated its technologies for X-ray inspection and visual web analytics.
