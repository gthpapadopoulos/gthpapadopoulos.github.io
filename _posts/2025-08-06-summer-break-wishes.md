The HUA computer vision group wishes everyone a wonderful and rejuvenating summer break, with the chance to relax, recharge and make great memories. During their summer time off, members of the group also visited the NASA Johnson Space Center in Houston, Texas. The visit offered inspiration from the tremendous scientific achievements of space exploration. The group looks forward to meeting everyone back refreshed after the summer.
