The kick-off meeting of the EU-funded KLEPTOTRACE project took place in Milan on June 8, 2023. The consortium aims to combat high-level corruption, by strengthening the investigation, tracing and recovery of assets linked to criminal activities. The Department of Informatics and Telematics leads the development of AI technologies that process massive amounts of data, in order to detect intricate corruption patterns, under the lead of G. Th. Papadopoulos.
