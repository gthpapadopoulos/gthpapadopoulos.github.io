G. Th. Papadopoulos participated in the Projects to Policy Seminar (PPS), organized by DG HOME in Brussels for EC projects funded under Cluster 3 on security. In particular, he mainly participated in the disaster response discussions, representing the TRIFFID project among others. The seminar included interesting discussions with policymakers and practitioners, as well as networking activities with other projects.
