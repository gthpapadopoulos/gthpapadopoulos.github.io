On March 27 and 28, the HUA research team presented scientific results of the Ceasefire project during the “Open Days HUA 2024” event, organized by Harokopio University of Athens for secondary school students. The presentation included live demos of the developed computer vision technologies. These concern X-ray-based illicit object detection, as well as image classification for illegal firearms trafficking.
