After the first group meeting of the new year, the HUA computer vision group honored the Greek custom of cutting the New Year's cake. According to the tradition, a pie (the so-called “Vasilopita”) with a hidden coin is cut into pieces, while the one who finds the coin is said to have luck during the new year. The event offered a nice team bonding moment, together with best wishes for a fruitful 2026.
