The study “Illicit object detection in X-ray imaging using deep learning techniques: A comparative evaluation” was accepted at IEEE Access. Automated X-ray inspection is crucial for efficient and unobtrusive security screening in public settings. The study systematically compares deep learning detectors for this task, while releasing its code along with demo results. It was funded by the Ceasefire project, and it is authored by J. Cani, C. Diou, S. Evangelatos, V. Argyriou, P. Radoglou-Grammatikis, P. Sarigiannidis, I. Varlamis and G. Th. Papadopoulos.
