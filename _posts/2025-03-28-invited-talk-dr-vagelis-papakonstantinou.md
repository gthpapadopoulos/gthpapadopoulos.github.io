Dr. Vagelis Papakonstantinou, Professor at Vrije Universiteit Brussel, gave an invited talk at the Department of Informatics and Telematics on “The Regulation of Digital Technologies in the EU”. The talk concentrated on phenomena that are common to all EU regulatory initiatives on digital technologies, namely act-ification, GDPR mimesis and regulatory brutality. These phenomena serve as early signs of a new regulatory approach to digital technologies in the EU.
