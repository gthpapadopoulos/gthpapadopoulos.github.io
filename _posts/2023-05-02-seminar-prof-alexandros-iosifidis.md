Within the weekly research seminars of the postgraduate programme of the Department of Informatics and Telematics, Prof. Alexandros Iosifidis gave an online lecture in Greek on “Human activity recognition in videos”. The lecture took place on Thursday, May 4, at 17:30 (Greece time), with free online participation. It presented modern methods for the recognition of human activities in video data.
