G. Th. Papadopoulos started a new position as expert evaluator at the European Commission. In particular, he serves as an evaluator for the European Research Executive Agency (REA), concerning research proposals under Cluster 3 of Horizon Europe on “Civil security for society”. The role builds on his long experience in EU-funded security research, as well as in the coordination and technical management of related projects.
