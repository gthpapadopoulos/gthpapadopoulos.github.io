The review “Multimodal Explainable Artificial Intelligence: A Comprehensive Review of Methodological Advances and Future Research Directions” is now available as a preprint. The study systematically analyzes the landscape of multimodal explainable AI, taking into account criteria such as the number of involved modalities and the stage at which explanations are produced. Moreover, it discusses the future research directions of the field.
