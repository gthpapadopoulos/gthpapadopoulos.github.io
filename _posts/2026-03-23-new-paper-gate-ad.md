The work “GATE-AD: Graph Attention Network Encoding For Few-Shot Industrial Visual Anomaly Detection” is now available as a preprint. GATE-AD follows a reconstruction-based approach to few-shot industrial visual anomaly detection. In contrast to commonly used encoders, it employs a graph attention network encoder, which is composed of stacked masked self-attentional layers. The work is authored by A. Psiris, Y. Panagakis, M. Vakalopoulou and G. Th. Papadopoulos, within the TORNADO project.
