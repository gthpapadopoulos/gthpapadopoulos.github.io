Dr. Maria Kyrarini, Assistant Professor at Santa Clara University, visited the Department of Informatics and Telematics for an invited talk. The talk concerned “Human-Centered Assistive Robots: From Understanding Commands to Detecting Cognitive Fatigue”. An interesting Q&A session and stimulating ideas, as well as bilateral group discussions, complemented the day, while further synergies are expected. The visit also strengthened the links between the HUA computer vision group and Santa Clara University.
