G. Th. Papadopoulos participated in the SEEDA-CECNSM 2026 conference, held at the Aristotle University Research Dissemination Center (KEDEA) in Thessaloniki, Greece. Joint works were presented on the forensic readiness of agentic AI and on synthetic investigation data. The first one, “HANSARD: A Reference Architecture for Forensic Readiness, Runtime Witnessing, and Graded Attribution in Autonomous Multi-Agent AI Systems”, is authored by C. Sardianos et al. The second one, “CaseBundleGen: LLM-Driven Generation of Synthetic Multimodal Data Lake Cases”, is authored by C. Panagiotopoulos et al.
