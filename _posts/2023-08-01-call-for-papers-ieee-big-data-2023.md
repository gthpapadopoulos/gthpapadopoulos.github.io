The workshop “Big Data applications for fight against crime and terrorism” will take place at the IEEE International Conference on Big Data 2023, on December 15-18, 2023, in Sorrento, Italy. The workshop is supported by the Ceasefire project. Relevant topics include big data applications, machine learning models and virtual assistants, as well as machine vision for security. The submission deadline is October 1, 2023.
