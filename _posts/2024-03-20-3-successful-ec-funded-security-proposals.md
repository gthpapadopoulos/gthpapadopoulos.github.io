In the recent call for EC-funded security proposals, G. Th. Papadopoulos was involved in successful proposals, namely ARMADILLO (HORIZON-CL3-2023-FCT-01-02) on the detection of GHB substances, GANNDALF (HORIZON-CL3-2023-FCT-01-06) on cybercrime detection and investigation, as well as TRIFFID (HORIZON-CL3-2023-DRS-01-05) on autonomous robotic aid for first responders. The projects extend the security research activities of the HUA computer vision group.
