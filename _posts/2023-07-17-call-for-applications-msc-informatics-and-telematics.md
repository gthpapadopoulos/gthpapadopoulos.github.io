Applications are open for the MSc programme “Informatics and Telematics” of the Department of Informatics and Telematics of Harokopio University, until September 8. The programme offers specialization in cutting-edge areas of informatics, while preparing its graduates for careers in industry and research. It is addressed to graduates who wish to deepen their knowledge in modern computing technologies.
