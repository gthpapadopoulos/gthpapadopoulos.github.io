The study “Illicit object detection in X-ray imaging using deep learning techniques: A comparative evaluation” is now available as a preprint. Automated X-ray inspection is crucial for efficient and unobtrusive security screening in public settings, although object occlusion and clutter remain major challenges. The study systematically compares deep learning detectors on public X-ray datasets, while releasing its code. It is authored by J. Cani, C. Diou, S. Evangelatos, V. Argyriou, P. Radoglou-Grammatikis, P. Sarigiannidis, I. Varlamis and G. Th. Papadopoulos.
