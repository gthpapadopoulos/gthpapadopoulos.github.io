A technical description of the technologies behind the Ceasefire platform is now available as a preprint. The platform tackles various types of cyber- and physical-world firearms trafficking, making use of advanced AI technologies. The manuscript targets an audience without extensive IT background. It mainly presents the platform modules, as well as the use cases they support.
