Multiple papers co-authored by G. Th. Papadopoulos were accepted at IEEE Big Data 2024, within the “2nd workshop on Big Data Applications for Fight against Crime and Terrorism”. The workshop is organized by the Ceasefire project. The works investigate different aspects of data collection, processing and visualization in security applications. These range from entity extraction to vulnerability assessment, as well as visual analytics for law enforcement.
