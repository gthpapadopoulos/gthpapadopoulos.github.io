G. Th. Papadopoulos will give an invited talk at Santa Clara University (Silicon Valley, California) on “Foundation Models in robotics”. The talk investigates holistically the usage of foundation models in robotics. It introduces their fundamental principles and concepts, as well as the evolution of robotic foundation model research. Moreover, it discusses the main learning methodologies, the robotic tasks addressed, and the open challenges, based on the work of the TORNADO project.
