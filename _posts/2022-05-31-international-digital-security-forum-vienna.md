G. Th. Papadopoulos was a speaker at the International Digital Security Forum (IDSF) Vienna 2022. The presentation concentrated on the recent advances of the ISFP EC-funded project Anti-FinTer. In particular, it introduced a novel approach, termed “follow-the-actor”, for conducting financial investigations in the Dark Web using advanced visual analytics and artificial intelligence technologies. The forum gathered experts from law enforcement, industry and academia on digital security.
