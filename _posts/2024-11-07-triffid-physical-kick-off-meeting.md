Harokopio University of Athens hosted the physical kick-off meeting of the TRIFFID project (auTonomous Robotic aId For increasing FIrst responDers efficiency), coordinated by G. Th. Papadopoulos. TRIFFID maximizes the efficiency of the field operations of first responders, by advancing remote and smart robotic reconnaissance in disaster sites. Its core goal is a hybrid legged UGV and UAV robotic platform for autonomous real-time inspection. The system will be demonstrated in wildfire, flood and earthquake scenarios.
