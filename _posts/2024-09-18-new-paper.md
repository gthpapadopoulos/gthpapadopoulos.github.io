The article “Distributed Maze Exploration Using Multiple Agents and Optimal Goal Assignment” was accepted at IEEE Access. Many robotic exploration approaches overlook real-world factors, such as broadcast range limitations, communication costs and coverage overlap. The study proposes a distributed exploration strategy (CU-LVP), which assumes constrained broadcast ranges and uses Voronoi diagrams for area partitioning. It is authored by M. Linardakis, I. Varlamis and G. Th. Papadopoulos.
