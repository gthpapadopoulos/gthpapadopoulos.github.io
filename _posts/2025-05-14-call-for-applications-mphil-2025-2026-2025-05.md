The call for applications to the Master of Philosophy (MPhil) in “Computer Science and Informatics” of the Department of Informatics and Telematics is open for the academic year 2025-2026. The programme targets candidates who wish to be involved in research activities, or to prepare for a follow-up PhD degree. Its main goal is to conduct high-level research in cutting-edge areas of computer science and informatics.
