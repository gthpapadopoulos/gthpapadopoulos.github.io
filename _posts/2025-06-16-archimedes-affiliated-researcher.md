G. Th. Papadopoulos joined the ARCHIMEDES Research Unit on AI, Data Science, and Algorithms as an affiliated researcher. His activities focus on the research areas of “Computer Vision and Robotics”, as well as “Machine Learning Foundations”. The affiliation strengthens the links of the HUA computer vision group with a leading Greek research hub in artificial intelligence.
