G. Th. Papadopoulos visited the headquarters of the Polícia de Segurança Pública (PSP) in Lisbon and, in particular, its National Firearms Focal Point (NFFP), within the Ceasefire project. The visit offered an interesting real-world case study on how artificial intelligence can facilitate the fight against firearms trafficking. Moreover, it supported the definition of the requirements for the Ceasefire technologies.
