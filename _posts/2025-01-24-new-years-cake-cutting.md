After a fruitful discussion with colleagues from other research organizations and industries on common research synergies, the HUA computer vision group honored the Greek custom of cutting the New Year's cake. According to the tradition, a pie (the so-called “Vasilopita”) with a hidden coin is cut into pieces, while the one who finds the coin is said to have luck during the new year. The event offered a nice team bonding moment.
