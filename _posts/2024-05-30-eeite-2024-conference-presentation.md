G. Th. Papadopoulos delivered a keynote speech at the International Conference in Electronic Engineering, Information Technology & Education (EEITE 2024), entitled “Deployment of computer vision technologies in the public security domain”. The presentation focused on the research results of the Department of Informatics and Telematics within security research projects. These mainly include large-scale visual analysis and X-ray image analysis.
