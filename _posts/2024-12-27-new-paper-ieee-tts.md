The article “The Invisible Arms Race: Digital Trends in Illicit Goods Trafficking and AI-Enabled Responses” was accepted at the IEEE Transactions on Technology and Society (IEEE TTS). The work analyzes how digital technologies transform illicit goods trafficking, as well as how AI can support law enforcement responses. It is authored by I. Mademlis, M. Mancuso, C. Paternoster, S. Evangelatos, E. Finlay, J. Hughes, P. Radoglou-Grammatikis, P. Sarigiannidis, G. Stavropoulos, K. Votis and G. Th. Papadopoulos.
