The proposal TORNADO, “foundaTion mOdels for Robots that haNdle smAll, soft and Deformable Objects”, was selected for funding by the EC under the competitive call HORIZON-CL4-2024-DIGITAL-EMERGING-01-03, with G. Th. Papadopoulos as the project coordinator. TORNADO develops a multi-functional and adaptive cloud robotics platform for autonomous mobile robots in complex, human-populated indoor environments. The platform largely leverages the zero-shot generalization abilities of robotic foundation models.
