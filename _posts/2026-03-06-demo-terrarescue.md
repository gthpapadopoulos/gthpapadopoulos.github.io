The video below presents TerraRescue, an artificial intelligence framework for ground-level panoptic image segmentation in post-disaster environments. TerraRescue equips unmanned ground vehicles (UGVs) with the semantic perception capabilities that are required for operation and navigation in complex, highly dynamic and cluttered environments. Such environments mainly include wildfire, flooding and earthquake sites. The solution was developed by the HUA computer vision group.
