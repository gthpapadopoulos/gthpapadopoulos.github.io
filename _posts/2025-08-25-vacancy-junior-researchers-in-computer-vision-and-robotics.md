The Department of Informatics and Telematics of Harokopio University of Athens offers multiple full-time vacancies for junior researcher positions in computer vision and robotics. The positions concern technologies such as SLAM, remote sensing, visual perception, as well as foundation models. The activities will focus on the application of AI and machine/deep learning solutions within EU-funded research projects. Applications are sent by e-mail to G. Th. Papadopoulos.
