The article “Applied Federated Model Personalization in the Industrial Domain: A Comparative Study” was published in the IEEE Open Journal of the Communications Society. The study proposes an advanced federated learning system that personalizes models in the industrial domain, while comparing alternative personalization strategies. The article is authored by I. Siniosoglou, V. Argyriou, G. Fragulis, P. Fouliras, G. Th. Papadopoulos, A. Lytos and P. Sarigiannidis.
