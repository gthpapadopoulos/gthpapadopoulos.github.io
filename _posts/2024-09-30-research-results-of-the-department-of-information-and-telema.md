Members of the HUA computer vision group participated in the seminar series “Research results of the Department of Informatics and Telematics”. They presented part of the research activities of the group and its active research projects. The presentations covered X-ray image analysis, optical remote sensing and foundation models in computer vision, as well as self-supervised learning and Raman spectroscopy image analysis.
