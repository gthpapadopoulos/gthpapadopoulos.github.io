The Centre for Continuing Education and Lifelong Learning of Harokopio University collaborated with CEPOL and Europol for delivering the first micro-credentials training on operational intelligence analysis. The Train-the-Trainers programme awarded micro-credentials (4 ECTS credits) to the participants who completed the course successfully. The course took place between October 20 and December 22, 2025. Moreover, it provides a nice example of collaboration between academic institutions and law enforcement agencies.
