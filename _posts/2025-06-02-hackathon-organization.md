A hackathon exercise was organized at the Department of Informatics and Telematics, within the undergraduate course “Programming II (Programming in C)”. The application domain of this year concerned fast parcel tracking systems, where the participants fulfilled individual missions based on real-world use-case scenarios. A dedicated online platform supported the exercises and evaluated the team responses automatically. Moreover, senior undergraduate students prepared and ran all hackathon materials.
