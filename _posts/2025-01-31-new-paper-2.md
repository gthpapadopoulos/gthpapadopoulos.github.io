The review “Advances in diffusion models for image data augmentation: a review of methods, models, evaluation metrics and future research directions” was accepted at Springer Artificial Intelligence Review. Image data augmentation is a critical methodology in modern computer vision, since it enhances the diversity and quality of training datasets. The review systematically analyzes diffusion-model-based approaches for this purpose. The article is authored by P. Alimisis, I. Mademlis, P. Radoglou-Grammatikis, P. Sarigiannidis and G. Th. Papadopoulos.
