G. Th. Papadopoulos joined Harokopio University of Athens as Assistant Professor at the Department of Informatics and Telematics. The academic community welcomed him on May 11, 2022, when he was sworn in before the Rector, Prof. Maria Nikolaidi. His position concerns computer graphics and computational vision, while his research focuses on computer vision and artificial intelligence.
