The partners of the TRIFFID project gathered in Rotterdam for the 2nd plenary meeting of the project. The first day covered progress updates across multiple work packages, open challenges, as well as the plan for the next months. A major highlight was the definition of the user requirements and use cases, namely wildfire at the wildland-industrial interface, urban flood response, and urban search and rescue after an earthquake.
