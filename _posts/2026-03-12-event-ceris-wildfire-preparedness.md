G. Th. Papadopoulos participated in the “Innovation uptake on wildfire preparedness” event, organized by the Community for European Research and Innovation for Security (CERIS) in Brussels. The event offered interesting ideas and discussions on the uptake of research outcomes by practitioners. In particular, it investigated how the work in the TRIFFID project can facilitate the actions of first responders during wildfires.
