G. Th. Papadopoulos participated in the Projects to Policy Seminar 2026, organized by DG HOME for newly funded projects under Cluster 3 of Horizon Europe. Together with E. Markakis, the project coordinator, he represented the SALUS project, also meeting the project officer G. Kaiafas. The seminar offered interesting discussions on the policy uptake of security research, as well as stimulating ideas for the next steps of the project.
