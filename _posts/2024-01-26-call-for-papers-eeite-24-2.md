The 5th International Conference in Electronic Engineering, Information Technology & Education (EEITE 2024) will take place on May 29-31, 2024, in Chania, Crete, Greece. It hosts the special session “Modern Biometric Technologies for Secure Authentication and Identity Verification”, supported by the TENSOR project. The paper submission deadline is February 29, 2024. Relevant topics cover biometric authentication and identity verification for security applications.
