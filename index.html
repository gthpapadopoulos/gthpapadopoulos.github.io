About

I received the Diploma and Ph.D. degrees in electrical and computer engineering from the Aristotle University of Thessaloniki (AUTH), Thessaloniki, Greece. I have worked as a Post-doctoral Researcher at the Foundation For Research And Technology Hellas / Institute of Computer Science (FORTH/ICS) and the Centre for Research and Technology Hellas / Information Technologies Institute (CERTH/ITI).

My research focuses on the areas of computer vision, artificial intelligence and machine/deep learning, with emphasis on robotics and security applications. In particular, my current research interests include, among others, foundation models for robotics, disaster robotics, self-supervised and generative visual learning, 3D scene reconstruction, remote sensing image analysis, as well as trustworthy and secure AI.

I have over 20 years of experience in participating in EU-funded research projects in the areas of ICT, security and robotics. Currently, I am the Principal Investigator (PI) of the EU-funded projects SALUS, TORNADO (Deputy Coordinator), TRIFFID (Coordinator), ARMADILLO and GANNDALF.
